## 1. Problem hypothesis
Fleet teams may manage vehicle history, tire records, workshop jobs, and expiry dates across separate spreadsheets and paper records. This can create follow-up burden and make it harder to review operational history. These are customer-discovery hypotheses, not measured market findings.

## 2. Solution
A centralized workspace intended to bring together:
- Fleet and vehicle records
- Preventive maintenance and service history
- Breakdown records
- Tire inventory and movement history
- Workshop job cards
- Vehicle-document issue and expiry dates
- Configurable document-expiry alerts
- Reporting and Excel/OneDrive integration workflows (validate live before claiming reliable real-time synchronization)

## 3. Illustrative workflow
1. Maintain a vehicle record.
2. Log a service, breakdown, tire, workshop, or document event.
3. Review the relevant module and history.
4. Use recorded dates and information to plan follow-up.

## 4. Initial customer segments
Trucking fleets, logistics and transportation companies, and independent workshops. Initial geography: Saudi Arabia, with international expansion considered after validation. These are target segments, not confirmed customers.

## 5. Business model hypothesis
Potential subscription tiers by fleet size and workflow needs: Starter, Growth, and Business. Pricing and willingness to pay are not yet validated.

## 6. Go-to-market
Direct outreach, guided pilots with agreed success criteria, and measurement of usage, task completion, record completeness, and willingness to pay.
