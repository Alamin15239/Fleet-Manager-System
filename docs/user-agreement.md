Operational records created or entered for Company work are subject to Company policies and recordkeeping requirements. ## 5. Maintenance and safety

Fleet Manager records and organizes information; it does not physically inspect vehicles, diagnose defects, certify roadworthiness, or replace manufacturer guidance, qualified mechanic judgment, or required safety checks. Users and the Company remain responsible for verifying records and making maintenance, dispatch, and safety decisions.

Alerts can be delayed or unavailable and are not emergency warnings. Scheduled checks target one daily run and do not provide continuous monitoring.

## 6. OneDrive and Excel

An administrator may connect an authorized Microsoft OneDrive account. Follow Company access rules for that account and workbook. Workbook edits enter the Excel Changes review queue and affect application records only after a permitted user applies them and server validation succeeds. Conflicts and invalid rows need review. Deleting a worksheet row does not delete an app record. App data may be written back to the connected workbook.

## 7. Job-card share links

A job-card link lets anyone who has the link read the shared card. Do not publish it or send it to an unintended recipient. It may reveal vehicle identifiers, driver or technician names, work, notes, and signatures. The Company may change or disable access to the application or shared record when needed.
