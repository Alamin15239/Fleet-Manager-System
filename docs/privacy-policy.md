# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026
Operator: Saadfreeh Transport Est. (“Company”, “we”, “us”)
Privacy contact: alaminkhan.xiaomi@gmail.com or the Company administrator

## 1. Scope

This policy describes the current handling of information in Fleet Manager, including the web app, APIs, user accounts, fleet/workshop records, reports, notifications, and connected OneDrive workbook. The Company controls information it enters or manages for its business. External providers process information as needed to provide their services and under their own terms.

## 2. Information handled

The application may handle:

- **Account information:** name, email, password hash, phone, department, title, biography, profile photo, role, permissions, approval and email-verification status, and password-recovery or verification data.
- **Fleet, workshop, and purchasing information:** truck and trailer identifiers, VIN, driver and mechanic names, mileage, status, maintenance and oil-change history, tire size and issue quantity, tire serials, job-card details, work/parts, vendor names and contact details, vendor invoice numbers and dates, expense categories, amounts, VAT details, fleet and vendor ledger entries, notes, attachments or signatures supplied to a card, and reports.
- **Security and activity information:** login history, actions and record changes, IP address, user agent, browser, operating system, device type, and approximate location derived from IP when available.
- **OneDrive integration information:** connected Microsoft account email, encrypted refresh token, workbook name/ID/link, sync status, and staged workbook changes.
- **Telemetry:** Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights data, and Microsoft Clarity page/interaction telemetry. The current Clarity integration identifies a signed-in session using the app user ID and sends the user role, email, and name as session metadata. There is currently no in-app analytics opt-out control.
- **Support and email information:** messages sent to support and email delivery details needed for verification, password recovery, tests, and enabled operational notifications.

Do not enter sensitive personal information in free-text fields unless the Company specifically requires it for a legitimate business purpose.

## 3. Purposes

Information is used to:

- create, authenticate, approve, secure, and recover user accounts;
- apply role-based and user-specific access controls;
- manage trucks, trailers, job cards, maintenance, oil changes, tires, vendor invoices, fleet expense accounts, VAT ledgers, reports, and Excel integration;
- deliver in-app notifications and configured emails;
- maintain activity/audit history, investigate incidents, and support users; and
- measure application usage and performance and improve reliability.

## 4. Access and disclosure

Authorized app users can see or change data according to their roles and individual permissions. Service providers receive data needed to operate the application:

- Vercel: application hosting, Analytics, and Speed Insights;
- Neon: PostgreSQL database hosting;
- Microsoft: OneDrive/Graph and Clarity;
- Resend: email delivery; and
- ip-api.com: approximate IP location lookup used by activity tracking.

The Company may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably needed to protect the service, Company, users, or others. The application is not operated as a data brokerage.

## 5. Job-card links and exports

Job-card share links are bearer links: anyone who obtains one can view the shared card without logging in. A card may show the job number, truck/trailer information, VIN or plate, driver or technician names, issue, work, checklist, notes, and signatures. Share only with intended recipients.

PDFs, reports, spreadsheets, and exported logs may contain personal or confidential business information. Protect them when downloading, storing, or sharing.

## 6. OneDrive and Microsoft data

When an administrator connects OneDrive, the account owner grants delegated Microsoft Graph access to the selected account for workbook integration. The app reads and updates the fleet workbook and stores the refresh token encrypted on the server. Workbook changes are staged for role-checked review before changing app records; app data may be written back to the connected workbook. Microsoft’s terms and privacy disclosures also apply to the connected account and files.

## 7. Storage location and international processing

The application uses PostgreSQL and external cloud services. The current production database endpoint is configured for a US East region. Vercel, Microsoft, Resend, and other providers may process information in other locations according to their service infrastructure and configuration. Data may therefore be processed outside Saudi Arabia.

The Company is responsible for assessing and applying the requirements and safeguards that apply under Saudi Arabia’s Personal Data Protection Law and the Regulation on Personal Data Transfer Outside the Kingdom.

## 8. Retention and deletion

The Company retains information for as long as needed to operate accounts, preserve fleet/workshop history, meet business and legal obligations, protect the service, and resolve disputes. Some records can be soft-deleted or kept in audit history. Backup copies may remain until the applicable backup cycle expires. The application does not publish one fixed retention period for every data type.

Contact the Company to request review, correction, or deletion. Applicable legal duties and operational recordkeeping requirements may limit deletion or require retention.

## 9. Choices and data-subject requests

You can update profile fields available in your account. You may contact the Company to request access to, a copy of, correction/completion/update of, or destruction of personal data, or to address consent where consent is the applicable legal basis. Requests are subject to identity verification, applicable law, and exceptions.

Send requests to alaminkhan.xiaomi@gmail.com or the Company administrator. Identify the account and request; do not send a password or access token.

## 10. Security

The application uses authentication, role and user permission checks, audit logging, password hashing, and server-side encryption for the OneDrive refresh token. No online service can promise absolute security. Keep credentials private and report suspected unauthorized access promptly.

## 11. Children, updates, and contact

Fleet Manager is a workforce business application and is not intended for children. This policy may be updated when the service or its data handling changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.

For questions or privacy requests, contact alaminkhan.xiaomi@gmail.com or the Company administrator.
